Each seeded store (the baseline's: a shared clock touched in every two-member group) gets durable
open checkpoints enabled and one tracked open whose drain persists a checkpoint at the head. Two
treatments follow, each the median of 5 opens in microseconds:
  checkpoint  the checkpoint at the head the previous process left; the provider answers Unchanged
  suffix      one seeded-sized group (2 events) appended after the checkpoint by a FullVerification
              writer; the open verifies that suffix
  start       RecordedProviderFacade::start_with_read_policy(.., ProviderTracked) on the SQLite file,
              the consumer's whole open, as in the baseline
  verify      EventlogRecordedStore::open_with_policy(.., ProviderTracked) on a provider handle
              opened for it, as in the baseline
Every measured facade shuts down with CancelQueued, which persists nothing, so every open of a
treatment starts from the same checkpoint. The probe asserts each open's OpenVerification, that the
store open takes no capture and builds no model, that a FullVerification open of the same store
still takes one capture and one whole-model build decoding every record, and the bound: the median
start at 1,203 events is at most twice the median at 55, for both treatments.
