The probe seeds the shared-clock stores shared_clock_cost.rs seeds (55, 601 and 1,203 events,
96-field metadata definition), then re-runs its own test binary once per shape and run, so each
measurement is a process of its own that seeds nothing. Each measuring process opens the SQLite
provider and attaches the projector, writes 5 to /proc/self/clear_refs, reads VmRSS (baseline),
opens one EventlogRecordedStore with CapturePolicy::ProviderTracked, and reads VmRSS and VmHWM.
It asserts the open was one capture, one whole-model build and every recorded entry decoded.
  live      VmRSS with the handle alive minus baseline
  peak      VmHWM minus baseline
  dropped   VmRSS after the handle is dropped minus baseline
peak and dropped equal live within 0.3 MB in every run: the allocator returned nothing during or
after the open, so live here is the open's resident peak, not its live heap.
Output below is the test's stdout from "running 1 test"; compiler output with local paths is omitted.

The change, in two steps:
  one record      the verified model's record, history and batch indexes, and the handle's
                  memory of verified histories, share one allocation per committed record
  final           in addition, the ProviderTracked open builds its model from the capture it
                  owns, moving each record blob into its record and each batch blob into the
                  allocation its batch holds instead of copying them, and the handle stops
                  retaining those blobs beside the model
